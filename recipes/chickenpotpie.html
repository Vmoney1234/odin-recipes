Cheddar Bay Biscuit Chicken Pot Pie

This isn't Colonel Sander's Chicken Pot Pie! If you are wanting a brilliant homemade dish, you have come to the right place. This recipe bring's a twist you are going to love. Everyone knows the delicacy of a Red Lobster Cheddar Bay Biscuit. Well... combine that garlic, buttery biscuit goodness with this comfort food staple and you have our world famous Chicken Pot Pie.

Ingredients

  • 1 Box Red Lobster Cheddar Bay Biscuit Mix
  • 1 can cream of chicken soup (10.5 oz.)
  • 4-5 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1 stick of salted butter
  • 1 bag of frozen mixed vegetables
  • 2 cups chicken broth/stock
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 cups whoe milk
  • 1 teaspoon of pepper
  • 1 teaspoon poultry seasoning
  • 2 cups whole milk

Directions

  1. First, preheat the oven to 350 degrees.
  2. Second, place the entire stick of butter in the 9x13 baking dish (casserole dish) and place it in the oven while it’s preheating to melt the butter.
  3. Third, mix the milk, Cheddar Bay Biscuit baking mix, salt, pepper, and the garlic seasoning packet in a large bowl.
  4. Next, layer the cooked, shredded chicken on top of the melted butter in the baking dish.
  5. In a separate mixing bowl add 1 can of cream of chicken soup, 2 cups of chicken broth, and the poultry seasoning and mix well.
  6. Then, pour this chicken soup mixture on the shredded cheese layer.
  7. Finally, pour the other half of the milk and biscuit mixture on top.
  8. Bake the chicken pot pie for 45 minutes or until the tops of the biscuits are golden brown. When you pull it out of the oven, it will have a little “jiggle” to it. Don’t be alarmed. It is finished cooking. You just need to let it sit for 8-10 minutes before you serve.
  9. Enjoy with your favorite sides! (Look below for some good ideas)
