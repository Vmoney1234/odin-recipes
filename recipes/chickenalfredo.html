Chicken Alfredo

Chicken Alfredo Only You're Nonna Could Make!

Imagine a brisk Saturday morning at your nonna's house. The smell of fresh air entering through the cracked windows, nonna's floured apron and her sweet smile welcoming you from the kitchen. She is rolling out her homemade pasta, chopping fresh herbs and prepping the breading for the chicken. Taking her time to create the perfect pasta dish while unknowingly creating unforgettable memories. The chicken alfredo she creates feels like a hug and tastes like seconds maybe even thirds. If you are interested in bringing this dish to your kitchen, lucky for you we have the recipe!

Ingredients

  • 1 boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 1 pound dry fettuccine noodles (1 box*)
  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic (minced)
  • 1¼ cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (from 1 lemon)
  • 1½ cups heavy cream (room temperature)
  • 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley

Directions

  1. Cut the chicken breast into thin strips. 1 boneless, skinless chicken breast
  2. Boil the fettuccine noodles according to the box instructions, drain, and set aside. 1 pound dry fettuccine noodles
  3. Heat a nonstick pan with the extra virgin olive oil and add the chicken strips. Cook on medium heat, for 6-7 minutes on each side until golden brown. Remove chicken from pan when ready and set aside. 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  4. Add the garlic and sauté for 3 minutes. Deglaze the pan with the chicken broth, add the fresh lemon juice, and bring to a boil.1 clove garlic, 1¼ cups low-sodium chicken broth, 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  5. Add the heavy cream, then add the cooked pasta and chicken. 1½ cups heavy cream
  6. Add the grated Parmesan cheese and stir until everything is well combined. 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  7. Sprinkle with fresh parsley and enjoy! ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley
